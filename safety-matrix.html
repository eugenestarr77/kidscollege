Classroom Safety Matrix 🛡️

This grid layout categorizes browser modules into transparent screening layers, sorting software by social risk vectors and advertisement footprint density parameters.

Understanding Our Safety Levels

🟢

Green Light (Safe & Instant Play)

These games are completely ad-free, contain no public chat functionality, and require zero setup. They run on isolated canvas mirrors or verified educational resources, making them safe to launch immediately on any device without unattended monitoring concerns.

Zero External Footprint
🟡

Yellow Light (Ad-Supported / Closed Rooms)

These games contain no open public keyboard or voice chat rooms, making them entirely safe from interaction risk vectors. However, they rely on commercial third-party web domains that deploy rotating layout ad boxes. Running an active browser ad-blocker filters these completely.

Ad-Blocker Recommended
🔴

Red Light (Private Lobbies Mandated)

These competitive arena matches feature unmoderated global matchmaking strings, text overlays, or aggressive video placements outside school profiles. In lab sessions, we strictly mandate that an instructor creates password-protected custom parties, completely isolating players from stranger entries.

Instructor Oversight Required

Lab Filtering Guidelines

External web game hosts can alter ad distribution tracking networks without warning. For the best security performance during recreational blocks, we recommend utilizing an active enterprise filter utility or local browser extension such as uBlock Origin to maintain clean canvas surfaces.
