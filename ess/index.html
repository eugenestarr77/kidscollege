Welcome to IMT136: Principles of Animation

Spring Semester

Welcome! This course covers fundamental 2D animation principles, processes, and creation techniques. Throughout the semester, online resources will serve as study companions to our primary text, The Animator's Survival Kit.

Course Structure & Pacing

This course is divided into 16 weeks. While most coursework does not require real-time attendance, you must adhere to scheduled weekly deadlines to stay on track. Please check the Course Calendar regularly to manage your time effectively.

Getting Started

  1. Review the course documents listed in this Start Here! module (Syllabus, Instructor Bio, and Required Software).
  2. Click on Lessons in the navigation bar above to access your weekly modules and assignment details.
  3. Utilize the SSC Resources menu at the top for institutional support services, including Disability Support Services and technical Helpdesk assistance.
