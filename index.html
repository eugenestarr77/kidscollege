Classroom Safety Matrix 🛡️

Our safety-first philosophy ensures that your child experiences play without compromise. This matrix categorizes browser games into transparent safety tiers.

Understanding Our Safety Levels

🟢

Green Light (Safe & Instant Play)

These games are completely safe for instant, independent, and unattended play. They are entirely ad-free, contain no public chat functionality, and require zero setup. They are safe to launch immediately on any device.

Zero Risk
🟡

Yellow Light (Ad-Blocker Recommended)

These games contain no open public chat rooms, making them socially safe. However, they are hosted on sites that run commercial, rotating banner advertisements. Utilizing an ad-blocker on your browser makes these completely clean for children.

Ad Blocker Advised
🔴

Red Light (Private Lobbies Mandated)

These games feature global matchmaking, unmoderated live chat lobbies, custom user-generated names, or aggressive video advertisements. Children should only play these if an instructor or parent has manually generated a Private Game/Party Room and provided the direct invite link, completely isolating them from public servers.

Supervisor Code Required

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the primary goal of the arcade educational? While many included sandbox builds foster advanced physical or logic skills (like *Neal.fun* or *Line Rider*), this site's ultimate objective is **tactile recreation, coordination building, and safe group play**.

How do you protect kids from rotating ads? These sites are hosted on external networks. Hosts can update ad widgets without our knowledge. Installing an ad-blocker, such as uBlock Origin, effectively filters out 99% of loading screens and banners.
