Parent Disclaimer & Lab Safety Notice

Welcome, parents! This catalog is curated by Eugene Starr as a safe repository for supervised recreation. Please review our safety notes to understand how the browser games operate:

🎮 The Core Goal is Play

While several of our sandbox puzzle games offer deep cognitive, scientific, or mathematical logic, the primary focus of this arcade is **active recreation and playground socialization**.

💥 Cartoon Action (No Blood)

Certain combat titles (like *Shellshock.io* or *SmashKarts*) feature shooting elements or explosions. All combat experiences are **entirely bloodless, cartoonish, and non-graphic**.

💬 Classroom Chat vs. Strangers

During class, kids use **private rooms and password protection** to play safely with their peers. If played at home outside of class, public servers will expose children to public, unmoderated chat lobbies with online strangers.

🛑 Rotating Ads & Safety

These sites are hosted on third-party domains. Some utilize rotating ads that we cannot filter. We strongly recommend installing an aggressive ad blocker like uBlock Origin on your child's browser.
